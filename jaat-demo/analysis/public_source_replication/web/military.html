Military titles → civilian occupations

Extend the occupational map to job titles

An occupation-level relatedness map can also connect job titles to civilian work. Here, established title-to-SOC crosswalks link military job titles to civilian occupations, which provide starting points in the relatedness map. Job descriptions supply task evidence for inspecting those connections.

  1. 1

    Build civilian profiles from postings

    TitleMatch maps posting titles to occupations; TaskMatch retrieves task IDs from posting text. We aggregate these into occupation-task profiles and compare their task weights to build the posting-task relatedness model.

  2. 2

    Extract tasks from military descriptions

    TaskMatch retrieves the same task IDs from official military duty descriptions. We compare these military task profiles with the civilian posting profiles. For three worked examples, we also review whether the military text supports the retrieved tasks.

  3. 3

    Connect job titles to the occupational map and compare with established crosswalks

    Task similarity connects military job descriptions to civilian occupation-task profiles. Compare these rankings with established military-to-civilian crosswalks, and open shared tasks to inspect the source text. Crosswalk matches also provide starting points for exploring related civilian occupations.

What this shows: job titles and descriptions can extend an occupation-level map. The crosswalk supplies documented connections; task similarity supplies a separate comparison. Neither establishes a verified career transition.

59% recover a documented civilian match

Among military entries with a crosswalk occupation covered by our civilian data, the task-based model includes a documented match among its top 20 recommendations for 59% (647 of 1,091).
