Build a profile from the descriptions you already have

Choose a collection of job descriptions or postings, code its titles and task/skill statements, and aggregate the retained evidence at the level useful to your institution. Keep links to source text so the resulting profile can be inspected and corrected.

Choose the unit

A job, team, agency or occupation answers a different question. Record the source period and what the corpus covers.

Code in a common language

Use TitleMatch, TaskMatch and SkillMatch for their distinct outputs. Record model, taxonomy and threshold versions.

Construct the signal

Distinguish common tasks from distinctive ones. State whether a percentage counts postings, task mentions or another unit.

Review and update

Inspect source evidence and suspect assignments. An advertised requirement is not a verified inventory of work performed.
