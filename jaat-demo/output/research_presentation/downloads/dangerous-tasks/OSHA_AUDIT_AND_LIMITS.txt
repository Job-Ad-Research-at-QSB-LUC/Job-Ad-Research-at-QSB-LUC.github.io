Corrections:
The historical extraction field pct_investigations used match occurrences divided
by reports. Here report percentages deduplicate investigation IDs. Historical
counts remain unvalidated retrieval counts, even for tasks whose membership was
subsequently reviewed. The evidence export also contains 93 context-only tasks;
these are labeled and excluded from the 610-task benchmark bundle.

Interpretation:
TaskMatch inference and AI judgments were not rerun. Review used purposive
retrieval and shared-family AI judgments, not representative expert validation.
The 610 tasks match O*NET 27.2; later taxonomy changes do not delete their evidence.
CFOI incorporates OSHA reports, so source independence is incomplete. Six CFOI
groups have incomplete constituent profile coverage. The weighted CFOI correlation
is 0.515 overall but 0.015 after removing SOC2 family mean ranks. Category samples
vary with BLS publication; categories can overlap. No causal task-injury links,
individual injury probabilities, or validated danger ranking are estimated.
The full research-profile bundle score includes identities that cannot be shown
publicly. Displayed top-ten task shares need not sum to that bundle score.
