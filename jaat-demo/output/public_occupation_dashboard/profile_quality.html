All existing benchmark eligibility, rankings, and results are retained. Flags concern the posting-task profiles and their interpreted links. They do not imply the observed transition benchmarks or published O*NET lists are wrong.

Automated screen of all SOC6 profiles and targeted aggregate-content review. Specialty badges inherit the parent SOC6 concern; they are not separate specialty judgments. Unflagged profiles are not certified. Original postings were not available for coding adjudication.

Screening rule: ten available task neighbors, at least eight in one other major occupational family, and less than 10% own-SOC6 canonical weighted task incidence. This is a review-selection rule, not a validity classifier. Additional targeted cases were inspected for weak occupational specificity. Canonical incidence is a share of task incidence, not a percentage of postings. Small samples and missing pair links alone do not establish miscoding.
