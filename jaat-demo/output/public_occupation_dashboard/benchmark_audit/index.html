Benchmark construction and reproduction

The comparisons evaluate fixed occupational recommendations against observed transition data. SST uses transition shares; EMSI uses the fraction of its original twenty ranked destinations recovered; survey comparisons use supplied transition weights. Mean recovery gives each origin occupation equal weight.

The equal-length comparison uses the smaller available recommendation list for each origin, capped at twenty. The up-to-twenty comparison permits different returned list lengths. Self-transitions are excluded, while observed outcomes outside the candidate population remain in recovery denominators.
