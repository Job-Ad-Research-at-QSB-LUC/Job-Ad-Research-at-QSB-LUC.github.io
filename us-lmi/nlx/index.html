Summary (NLx corpus)

NLx data disclaimer. This section uses aggregate evidence derived from the National Labor Exchange (NLx) corpus; raw NLx posting text is not public. The National Labor Exchange (NLx) Data Trust bear no responsibility for the analyses or interpretations of the data presented here. For more information, see NLx Research Hub.

Aggregate evidence from the NLx corpus. This top-level page is a hub for profilers and corpus-level checks using aggregate data from the national NLx corpus; USAJOBS-only federal evidence is kept in the USAJOBS section. The NLx corpus represents approximately 162 million source postings before monthly expansion; dashboard rates use monthly active posting-month rows.

How to read this section. NLx pages are national posting evidence. Main finding pages make curated claims; profiler pages are lookup tools for comparing occupations, industries, tasks, skills, and concepts.
