STEM and Green Task Bundles

This page compares official occupation labels with postings-derived task-bundle evidence in the NLx corpus. STEM is the main case: the task signal is large enough to support a fuller comparison between official O*NET/BLS STEM occupations and postings-derived STEM candidates. Green is included below as a smaller companion check.

NLx data disclaimer. The National Labor Exchange (NLx) Data Trust bear no responsibility for the analyses or interpretations of the data presented here.
Candidate convention. A postings-derived candidate is an occupation with above-average concentration of the task bundle (RCA > 1) and enough supporting evidence: at least 25,000 active jobs, 250 task mentions, 5 distinct task IDs, 0.08 task mentions per active job, and a 4% task share. Occupations with RCA > 1 that fail one or more of those checks are shown as “not enough evidence for candidate.” These are diagnostic posting measures, not replacements for official classifications.
