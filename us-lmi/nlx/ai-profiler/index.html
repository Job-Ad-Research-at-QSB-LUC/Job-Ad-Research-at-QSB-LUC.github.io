AI Profiler

NLx data disclaimer. The National Labor Exchange (NLx) Data Trust bear no responsibility for the analyses or interpretations of the data presented here.

AIMatch aggregates from the NLx corpus summarize occupation and industry exposure to AI-coded task and skill language.

How to read this page. This is an exploratory profile of AI intensity in national NLx postings. Strict AI codes are narrower; lenient and composite measures are broader. Rankings are most useful for larger occupation or industry cells and for score differences above the validation floor. The occupation and industry ranking tables require at least 30,000 active jobs from January 2021 through September 2025.
Validation note. Posting-level LLM validation supports ordering for score gaps of about 0.05 or larger, while aggregate rankings are most defensible for large cells. The external benchmark below compares broad NAICS-2 sector averages from NLx with Census BTOS reported firm AI use; it does not validate every detailed industry ranking.
